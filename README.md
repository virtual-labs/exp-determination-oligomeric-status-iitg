### About the Experiment

This experiment covers the preparation and packing of a size exclusion (gel filtration) chromatography column and its use to determine the oligomeric status of a given protein.
The cross-linked porous beads separate molecules by size: large molecules are excluded from the pores and elute early, while smaller molecules travel a longer path through the beads and elute later.
The column is calibrated with protein standards of known molecular weight to relate the elution volume and the distribution coefficient (Kd) to molecular size.
The native molecular weight of the given protein is read from the calibration curve and compared with its subunit mass to establish whether it is monomeric or oligomeric.
